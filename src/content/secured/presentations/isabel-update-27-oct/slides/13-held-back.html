Where we are held back

Detail before direction Many views and opinions. Talks go deep into details before the broad picture stands; we would fill those in as we meet them.
Different priorities Ours are not always those of others. We are still waiting on the VM to run an autonomous agent.
Ponto: the riskier release The Ruby upgrade is wanted before the test suite that makes it safe, chosen for local goals over the broad ones.
Two camps, one project Ops and dev decide apart where one team should carry it. Everything escalates to management, with no aim beyond each camp’s own priorities.
