01 What gates check, and what that brings

What they check
Team standards: code standards, agent guidelines and architecture rules per team. Correctness: unit and integration tests, a coverage threshold, evals in CI, API contract and migration checks, performance budgets and accessibility checks. Security and compliance: security, dependency and secret scans, no personal data in logs, and four-eyes approval with an audit trail. Review: an AI reviewer checks acceptance criteria, guidelines, bugs, vulnerabilities and performance, then a person reviews the merge request.
What that brings
No rogue AI: agents cannot overreach More trust in AI: its output is checked Better output: quality over a quick pass Standardisation: coverage and security
