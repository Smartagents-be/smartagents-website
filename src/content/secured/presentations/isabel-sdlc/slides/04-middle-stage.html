The middle stage is where the people change

It is the stage where a team can learn the new way of working while the stakes are still low.

Learning in phases Nobody goes from writing every line to reviewing an agent’s work overnight. This is the step in between.
Trusting the output Trust comes from working next to it on real code, not from a policy that says it is allowed.
Living with gates Once an agent writes the volume, a gate stops being bureaucracy and becomes what makes the volume safe.
