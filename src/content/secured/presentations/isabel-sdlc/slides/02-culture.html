The hardest constraint is not technical

Direct quotes.

“We have always done it this way” Answered by input from outside the team. Someone who has already made the move, in the room, not a memo about it.
“I do not trust it, my code is better” Answered by demonstration. A real repository, a real change, measured against what the team would have shipped. Evidence beats argument.
“We decided to not invest in AI this year” Then the gap widens for a year, and the codebase and the habits both get harder to change. Waiting has a price.
