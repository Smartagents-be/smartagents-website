What we need: a mandate across teams

Priority Platform engineering as the primary track, with Ponto and anti-fraud as pilot teams. Decided once, so it is not reopened team by team.
Resources More tokens and credits, on an economic model that leaves room to learn and experiment, so people get good at AI instead of rationing it. Plus service accounts so agents run on remote machines.
People A small, new platform team that owns the platform, and evangelists in the teams whose job is coaching and changing minds.
Feedback A standing loop back to us, so we act on what the teams actually run into, not on what we assumed.
