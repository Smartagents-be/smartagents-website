What we propose, in this order

The goal is to do more with the same people.

01 Quality gates Some teams already have strong gates, others far less. Every team reaches the same bar in CI/CD: today’s standards, or stricter.
02 Platform One way of working, owned by a small platform team and carried by the tooling instead of remembered by people.
03 Training Teams learn to work with agents rather than around them.

Access without gates buys you more code and less confidence in it.
