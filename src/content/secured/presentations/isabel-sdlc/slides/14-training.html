03 Where training pays off first

Beyond search AI is mostly used to look things up. The larger gain comes from handing it a whole task, end to end.
The right model Lighter, less expensive models do most everyday work very well. Knowing when to use which is a skill of its own.
Proven workflows Battle-tested workflows already exist for the common jobs. Knowing why they work lets a team adapt them to its own work.
What it costs The cost of an AI task is rarely visible, so it is hard to steer on. Once it is, the choices get easier.

The willingness is there. What is missing is knowing how to use AI well, and that is what training teaches.
