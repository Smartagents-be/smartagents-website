Where the session leaks tokens.

The infinite exploration
→“Investigate the codebase” with no scope reads hundreds of files into the window. Scope it, or send a subagent that explores in its own context and reports back just the answer.
The kitchen-sink session
→One session drifting across unrelated tasks; every later message reprocesses the junk. One task per session, /clear when it changes.
Filling up before compacting
→Quality drops as the window fills; waiting until it is nearly full bakes in the rot. /compact early, before the limit.
Correcting over and over
→After two failed fixes the thread is polluted with dead ends that distract the model. Reset with a sharper prompt; a fresh session beats more iteration.

Source: Anthropic, Claude Code best practices (code.claude.com/docs).
