Where the session leaks tokens.

The infinite exploration A prompt like investigate the codebase, with no scope, reads hundreds of files into the window. Scope it, or send a subagent that reports back the answer.
The kitchen-sink session One session drifting across unrelated tasks reprocesses the junk on every later message. One task per session, and compact early rather than when the window is nearly full.
Correcting over and over After two failed fixes the thread is full of dead ends that distract the model. A fresh session beats more iteration.
