The honest numbers

AI speeds things up, but not automatically.

55% faster on a scoped task: Copilot users finished in 1h11 against 2h41. And 19% slower for experienced developers on their own large, familiar codebase, while they were sure they were faster. About 1 in 5 packages named by open-source models do not exist, 1 in 20 for commercial ones, and attackers register the names.

GitHub Copilot productivity study 2022. METR 2025. Spracklen et al., USENIX Security 2025: 21.7% open-source, 5.2% commercial. DORA 2025 calls AI an amplifier: scope tight, and verify the output.
