What these tips give you.

Build for small context Small, single-purpose files and clear boundaries mean a change needs less of the codebase, and the structure does the scoping.
Plan before you code Agree the approach in prose first: a wrong plan is a sentence to fix, not a diff to revert.
Work in small steps One change at a time: smaller diffs, smaller context, easier review.
Pick the model for the task Default to the cheapest tier that clears the work, and dial the effort down before you reach for a bigger model.
