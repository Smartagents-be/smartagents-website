A 30-day plan to roll this out to your team.

Week 1 Measure and document Log your current token spend and response times as a baseline. Add an instruction file to each active project, so every prompt starts clean.
Weeks 2 and 3 Structure and habits Refactor one oversized module into smaller, single-purpose files and measure the token drop. Adopt plan-before-code on a real task, and pick the model deliberately.
Week 4 Standardize and share Agree team defaults: a rule file, plan first, the model picker. Share the before-and-after numbers, then pick the next module to improve.
