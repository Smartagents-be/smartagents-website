The anatomy of an LLM bill.

What you send Cheap, and the easiest to over-send
Input tokens: the system prompt, the context, the history, the files. The cheapest type. Cached input tokens: about 0.1x input, 90% off, when a stable prefix repeats. Identical system prompts and reused context qualify.
What you pay for Where the money is
Output tokens: roughly 3 to 6x input, depending on the model. The expensive side of the bill. Reasoning tokens: hidden thinking steps, billed as output. A long chain on a short question can dwarf the visible answer.
