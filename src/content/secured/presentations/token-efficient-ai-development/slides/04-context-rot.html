Mental model

More context is not a better answer.

Chroma tested 18 models in July 2025, from GPT-4.1 to Claude 4, Gemini 2.5 and Qwen3: the same question is answered far more reliably from a focused prompt of about 300 tokens than from the full context of about 113K. Attention is spread across every token you send, and what sits in the middle is missed most often, so put what matters at the top or the bottom.
