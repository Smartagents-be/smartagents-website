Pick the model for the task.

Small and fast Haiku, Gemini Flash, a mini or nano model. Renames, boilerplate, simple edits and quick questions. Cheapest by far.
Mid-tier Sonnet, GPT-5.4. The default workhorse for most coding: enough reasoning for real features, at a sane price.
Top tier Opus, Fable, GPT-5.5. Reserve it for genuinely hard reasoning, tricky debugging and architecture, where quality earns the cost.

Changing the effort is often a better lever than changing the model.
