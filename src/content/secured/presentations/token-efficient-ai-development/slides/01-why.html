Tokens are money, time and quality.

Every token costs You pay per token, and output is three to six times more expensive than input. Waste adds up fast across thousands of requests.
More context is not better Models lose focus as the context grows. Sending too much lowers the quality of the answer.
Slower and more expensive Unnecessary context wastes tokens and slows down both the agent and your review of the result.

Token efficiency is not a trade-off. The same approach lowers your costs and improves your output.
