Where does AI deliver the most value per token?

Low Complex legacy Large, familiar systems where the context a change needs is the whole problem.
Medium Code review and debugging Real help on work you were going to do anyway, as long as you read what comes back.
High Scoped and repeatable work Small, scoped features. Boilerplate. Planning and docs.

High value on scoped, repeatable work. Low value in large, familiar codebases. Always verify the output.
