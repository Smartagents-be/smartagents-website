Keep your agent’s work area clean.

Scope what the agent sees Point it to the right files yourself, or give it a focused starting point and let it find the rest. Exclude noise with an ignore file. Every tool does this a little differently.
Turn off unused tools Every connected MCP server loads its tool definitions into the context, even unused ones. Review your active servers, /mcp in Claude Code or Settings then MCP in Cursor, and turn off what this task does not need.
Check your client’s limits Cursor historically capped the tools sent to the model at around 40. Treat any fixed number as out of date and check what your client does today.

A focused context is faster, cheaper and more accurate than a full one. Paste only the relevant error line, not entire logs.
